## Purpose of this folder

This folder holds the first client-ready **productization kit** for the People 404™
Work Health Check — the first packaged diagnostic offer under Sustineri Consulting Group.

The kit turns a concept ("map how work actually gets done vs. the org chart") into
something a founder can sell, deliver, and repeat.

## Product summary

**People 404™ Work Health Check** compares the formal org chart to how work actually gets
done. It surfaces hidden dependency risk, overloaded roles, undocumented work, bottlenecks,
informal repair labor, and AI/automation readiness gaps.

> **Your org chart shows authority. People 404 maps dependency.**

The formal org chart shows who reports to whom. The **actual work chart** shows how the
organization survives. People 404 maps the gap between them.
