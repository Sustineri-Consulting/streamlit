# People 404™ Work Health Check — Client Intake Form

**How to use:** Send to the client after the fit call. Answers can be short. If a question
doesn't apply, write "N/A." There are no wrong answers — the gaps are part of the finding.

---

## 1. Organization basics

- Organization name:
- Industry / what you do:
- How long in operation:
- Total headcount (full-time / part-time / contractors):
- Locations (physical, remote, hybrid):
- Who is filling out this form, and their role:

## 2. Team structure

- How is the team organized (departments, pods, no formal structure)?
- How many managers / team leads?
- Any recent changes (growth, layoffs, reorg, leadership change) in the last 12 months?
- Any changes expected in the next 12 months?

## 3. Current org chart

- Do you have a current org chart? (yes / no / roughly)
- Please attach it or describe it.
- How accurate is it, honestly? (very / somewhat / out of date)

## 4. Key roles

- List the roles the business could not run a week without:
- For each, who currently fills it?
- Are any of these roles held by only one person?

## 5. Critical workflows

- What are the 3–5 most important things that must happen every week for you to operate?
- For each, roughly who touches it and what tool is used?
- Which of these would customers notice immediately if it stopped?

## 6. Recurring problems

- What breaks or slows down most often?
- What "fire" keeps coming back?
- Where do things fall through the cracks?

## 7. Who fixes what

- When something breaks, who does everyone actually go to?
- Is that the person whose job it officially is? (yes / no / sometimes)
- Who ends up doing work that isn't in their job description?

## 8. Tools and systems

- List the main tools/software you rely on (CRM, scheduling, billing, comms, etc.):
- Which tools are "load-bearing" — a real problem if they went down?
- Any tool only one person truly understands?

## 9. Vendors

- List outside vendors/contractors you depend on:
- Which would hurt most to lose?
- Any single vendor with no backup?

## 10. Documentation

- What is written down (processes, SOPs, playbooks)?
- What is not written down but should be?
- If a key person left tomorrow, what knowledge leaves with them?

## 11. Turnover / absence risk

- Any roles hard to hire or replace?
- Anyone been talking about leaving, retiring, or cutting back?
- What happens today if a key person is out for 2 weeks?

## 12. AI / automation interest

- Are you considering AI or automation anywhere? Where?
- What repetitive work would you most like to reduce?
- Any concerns about automating (quality, trust, compliance)?

## 13. What leadership is worried about

- What keeps you up at night about how the business runs?
- What's the "if this person left, we'd be in trouble" situation?
- What do you wish you had clearer visibility into?

## 14. What staff may know that leadership does not see

- Where might the day-to-day reality differ from what leadership assumes?
- Any workarounds people quietly rely on?
- Is there anyone we should talk to who "just knows how things work"?

---

**Attachments welcome:** org chart, process docs, tool list, vendor list, recent incident
notes. The more real material, the sharper the map.
