# People 404™ Product Ladder & Pricing

**Owner:** Sustineri Consulting Group · **Created:** 2026-07-01 · **Status:** Draft (founder review)

> Your org chart shows authority. People 404 maps dependency.

Three tiers. Same core idea at every level: compare the **formal org chart** to the
**actual work chart**, and map the gap. The tiers differ in depth, access, and output.

---

## Tier 1 — Signal Map

**Price range:** $1,500 – $2,500

**Buyer type:** Small teams, solo founders, early buyers who want a fast, low-risk look.

**What it includes:**
- Intake review
- One connective map
- Top 5 hidden risks
- 30-day action list
- Short findings memo

**Client receives:**
- Signal Map
- Risk Snapshot
- 30-Day Fix List

**Delivery timeline:** ~1 week from completed intake.

**Upsell path:** Signal Map credit can apply toward a Work Health Check within 60 days.

---

## Tier 2 — People 404™ Work Health Check (flagship)

**Price range:** $3,500 – $7,500

**Buyer type:** Growing teams, clinics, agencies, professional service firms, operators who
sense concentration risk but can't map it.

**What it includes:**
- Intake
- Stakeholder review
- Critical role map
- Dependency map
- Actual work chart
- Formal chart vs. actual work comparison
- Hidden risk findings
- Documentation gap review
- AI / automation readiness review
- 60-minute readout

**Client receives:**
- Full findings report
- Connective map
- Actual work chart
- 60-day repair plan

**Delivery timeline:** 2–4 weeks.

**Upsell path:** Findings that need hands-on fixing → Human Infrastructure Report, or a
scoped repair/implementation engagement.

---

## Tier 3 — Human Infrastructure Report

**Price range:** $10,000+

**Buyer type:** Larger or higher-risk organizations; regulated environments; pre-transition
(sale, funding, succession, leadership change).

**What it includes:**
- Everything in the Work Health Check
- Interviews
- Workflow review
- Documentation review
- Vendor / process dependency review
- Risk scoring
- 90-day implementation roadmap

**Client receives:**
- Human Infrastructure Report
- Risk scoring
- Leadership readout
- 90-day repair roadmap

**Delivery timeline:** 4–8 weeks.

**Upsell path:** Ongoing advisory / retained repair support after the roadmap.

## When to discount / when not to

**Reasonable to discount or offer credit:**
- Signal Map credited toward a later Work Health Check (planned upsell, not a giveaway).
- First reference client willing to give a real, usable testimonial or case study.
- Nonprofit / early-stage founder where the relationship has clear future value.

**Do not discount:**
- To win a buyer who won't share documents or give access — that guarantees a weak
  diagnostic and an unhappy client.
- Because the buyer says "it's just a chart." The value is the analysis, not the artifact.
- Below the floor of a tier. If the budget only fits a Signal Map, sell the Signal Map —
  don't deliver a Work Health Check at Signal Map pricing.
- To rush the timeline. Compressed access produces shallow findings; protect the quality bar.

**Note:** Prices are ranges pending founder sign-off. Set the specific number per engagement
based on team size, access complexity, and risk level — document the number in the tracker.
