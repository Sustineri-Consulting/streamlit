# People 404™ Work Health Check — Delivery SOP

**Purpose:** The repeatable, step-by-step process for delivering a Work Health Check so
quality stays consistent and the productization loop actually closes.

**Default owner:** Lead consultant (Angeline / Sustineri). Adjust per engagement.

---

## Phase 1 — Pre-sale qualification

- **Owner:** Lead consultant
- **Inputs:** Inbound interest, fit call
- **Steps:** Confirm the buyer is a fit (see Offer Sheet best-fit / not-a-fit). Confirm
  they'll share documents and give access. Select tier. Agree price within range.
- **Outputs:** Agreed tier + price, signed engagement/agreement, kickoff date
- **Quality check:** Not-a-fit criteria reviewed and cleared
- **Proof:** Signed agreement logged in tracker

## Phase 2 — Intake

- **Owner:** Lead consultant
- **Inputs:** Client Intake Form, Critical Role Questionnaire
- **Steps:** Send both. Chase for completion. Book stakeholder review (Tier 2+) / interviews (Tier 3).
- **Outputs:** Completed intake + questionnaire responses
- **Quality check:** No critical section left blank without a reason
- **Proof:** Completed forms stored in the engagement folder

## Phase 3 — Document collection

- **Owner:** Lead consultant + client point of contact
- **Inputs:** Org chart, SOPs, tool list, vendor list, incident notes
- **Steps:** Collect and inventory. Note what's missing (missing docs are findings).
- **Outputs:** Document inventory
- **Quality check:** Every "load-bearing" tool/vendor named in intake is accounted for
- **Proof:** Document inventory list

## Phase 4 — Role mapping

- **Owner:** Lead consultant
- **Inputs:** Intake, questionnaire, documents
- **Steps:** Fill the Critical Role Map. Score roles (SPOF, undocumented, overload, absence impact).
- **Outputs:** Scored critical role map
- **Quality check:** Scoring matches evidence, not assumption

## Phase 5 — Dependency mapping

- **Owner:** Lead consultant
- **Inputs:** Role map, documents, interviews
- **Steps:** Complete the Connective Map Template. Build the actual work chart. Note formal-vs-actual gaps.
- **Outputs:** Completed connective map + actual work chart
- **Quality check:** Every High/Critical row has a failure impact and a repair recommendation

## Phase 6 — Analysis

- **Owner:** Lead consultant
- **Inputs:** All artifacts above
- **Steps:** Identify hidden risks, bottlenecks, documentation gaps, AI/automation readiness.
  For Tier 3, produce risk scoring.
- **Outputs:** Findings set + prioritized risks
- **Quality check:** Findings are specific and evidence-backed, not generic advice

## Phase 7 — Report drafting

- **Owner:** Lead consultant
- **Inputs:** Findings, Report Template
- **Steps:** Populate the Report Template. Write the 30/60(/90)-day repair plan. Draft the
  recommended next engagement.
- **Outputs:** Draft report + connective map + repair plan
- **Quality check:** Executive summary readable in one minute; no unsupported claims

## Phase 8 — Founder review

- **Owner:** Founder (Angeline)
- **Inputs:** Draft report, Founder Review Checklist
- **Steps:** Run the checklist. Verify claims/proof, scope, pricing, brand, compliance caution.
- **Outputs:** Approved report
- **Quality check:** All checklist items pass; no overpromising

## Phase 9 — Client readout

- **Owner:** Lead consultant
- **Inputs:** Approved report
- **Steps:** 60-minute readout. Walk the map, the top risks, the repair plan. Answer questions.
- **Outputs:** Delivered report + recorded/summarized readout
- **Quality check:** Client can restate their top risk and first action in their own words

## Phase 10 — Follow-up offer

- **Owner:** Lead consultant
- **Inputs:** Recommended next engagement from the report
- **Steps:** Present the natural next step within a week. Tie it to a specific finding.
- **Outputs:** Follow-up proposal
- **Quality check:** Offer is specific, not a generic upsell

## Phase 11 — Archive and tracker update

- **Owner:** Lead consultant
- **Inputs:** All engagement artifacts
- **Steps:** Archive the engagement folder. Update the Productization Tracker and any
  Sustineri OS trackers. Record outcome and next action.
- **Outputs:** Archived engagement, updated trackers
- **Quality check:** Tracker reflects final status and next action

---

## When the loop is considered closed

The delivery loop is **closed** for an engagement only when:

- [ ] Report delivered and approved (founder review passed)
- [ ] Readout completed
- [ ] Repair plan handed over
- [ ] Follow-up offer made
- [ ] Engagement archived
- [ ] Tracker(s) updated with outcome + next action

If any item is incomplete, the loop is **Partial** — record the blocker and the next action.
