# Critical Role Questionnaire

**Purpose:** Identify the roles — and people — the organization cannot function without.
This is where hidden dependency shows up. Use alongside the Client Intake Form.

**How to use:** Ask a few leaders and, ideally, a few staff separately. Differences between
their answers are a finding, not a mistake.

---

## A. Who owns critical work

1. What are the tasks that absolutely cannot slip? Who owns each one?
2. For each critical task, is there a backup who could do it well? Name them.
3. Which critical tasks have no real backup?

## B. Who people go to when something breaks

4. When [billing / scheduling / a client crisis / the system going down] happens, who is
   the first person people call?
5. Is that person's name in the official process? (yes / no)
6. Who is the "unofficial helpdesk" everyone leans on?

## C. Who knows undocumented processes

7. What important processes live only in someone's head?
8. Who is the only person who knows how to do a specific critical thing?
9. If we asked that person to write it down, how long would it take — and has anyone asked?

## D. Who holds passwords / access / process memory

10. Who controls access to critical accounts, systems, or vendors?
11. Is access shared or documented anywhere secure? (yes / no)
12. Who remembers "why we do it this way" — the history behind the current setup?

## E. Who is overloaded

13. Who is clearly doing more than one role's worth of work?
14. Who gets pulled into everything?
15. Whose calendar/inbox is the real bottleneck?

## F. Who is quietly keeping operations alive

16. Who fixes problems before anyone else notices?
17. Who does the "glue work" — coordinating, reminding, cleaning up — that isn't on any chart?
18. If that person stopped doing the invisible parts, what would break first?

## G. Who would cause disruption if absent for 2 weeks

19. Name the people whose 2-week absence would cause real problems. What breaks for each?
20. Whose absence would be barely noticed operationally? (Also useful to know.)
21. Has a key person's absence ever caused a scramble? What happened?

## H. Who is missing from formal decision-making

22. Who has real influence over how work happens but isn't in official decision meetings?
23. Whose input is needed to make a decision stick, even if they're not "in charge"?
24. Where do decisions get made informally, off the org chart?

---

## Scoring aid (for the analyst)

For each named person/role, rate 1 (low) to 5 (high):

| Person / role | Single point of failure | Undocumented knowledge | Overload | Absence impact | Notes |
|---------------|-------------------------|------------------------|----------|----------------|-------|
| | | | | | |

High scores across multiple columns = a **critical role** to flag in the connective map
and report.
