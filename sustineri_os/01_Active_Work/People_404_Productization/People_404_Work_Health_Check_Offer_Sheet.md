# People 404™ Work Health Check — Offer Sheet

**Owner:** Sustineri Consulting Group · **Created:** 2026-07-01 · **Status:** Draft (founder review)

## Product name

**People 404™ Work Health Check**

## One-line positioning

> Your org chart shows authority. People 404 maps dependency.

## Who it is for

Founders, small business owners, operators, HR leaders, clinic owners, agency owners,
contractors, professional service firms, and growing teams — organizations that depend on
a handful of people to keep running and can't clearly see who those people are.

## Problem it solves

The org chart says who reports to whom. It does not show how work actually gets done. When
the real work lives in a few heads, a few inboxes, and a few undocumented routines, the
organization is one absence away from a stall — and leadership usually can't see it until
it breaks.

People 404 finds the gap between the formal org chart and the **actual work chart** before
it becomes an outage, a burnout, or a failed handoff.

## What is included

- Structured intake and document review
- Stakeholder review
- Critical role map (who the org cannot function without)
- Dependency map / connective map
- Actual work chart (how work really flows)
- Formal chart vs. actual work comparison
- Hidden risk findings (single points of failure, bottlenecks, informal repair labor)
- Documentation gap review
- AI / automation readiness review
- 60-minute readout

## What the client receives

- Full findings report
- Connective map
- Actual work chart
- 60-day repair plan

## Timeline

Typically **2–4 weeks** from signed intake to readout, depending on team size and how fast
documents and interviews are made available.

## Pricing range

**$3,500 – $7,500** (flagship tier). See the Product Ladder for the lighter Signal Map
($1,500–$2,500) and the deeper Human Infrastructure Report ($10,000+).

## Best-fit buyer

- 5–150 people, or a critical unit within a larger org
- Recently grew, reorganized, lost key staff, or is about to
- Leadership senses "we'd be in trouble if [person] left" but can't map it
- Considering AI/automation and wants to know what's safe to automate

## Not-a-fit buyer

- Wants a staffing plan, recruiting, or an HR compliance audit (different product)
- Wants software installed or a system built (this is a diagnostic, not a build)
- Won't share documents or give access to staff
- Looking for the cheapest possible checkbox exercise
