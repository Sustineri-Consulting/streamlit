# Internal SOP — People 404™ Work Health Check Delivery

**Offer:** People 404™ Work Health Check — $3,500 launch price
**Owner:** Founder (founder-led delivery)
**Purpose:** A repeatable, founder-led process to deliver the Work Health Check with
consistent quality from intake through follow-up.

> Internal use only. Do not publish. The public site links only the sample deliverable.

---

## Stage 1 — Intake review

Confirm the submitted intake is complete and understand the buyer's situation before any call.

**Must be reviewed before proceeding:**
- Organization, size, industry, buyer name + title
- Stated reason for the Work Health Check and desired outcome
- Signals across: operational strain, AI status, growth pressure, burnout/turnover,
  role clarity, knowledge transfer, process breakdowns, decision bottlenecks, comms gaps
- Urgency level and follow-up consent

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Intake received and complete (chase missing critical fields)
- [ ] Buyer authority to engage confirmed
- [ ] Consent to follow-up recorded
- [ ] Initial read of the top 3 likely pressure points noted

## Stage 2 — Fit check

Decide whether the Work Health Check is the right offer, a referral, or a larger engagement.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Problem is a workforce-infrastructure problem (fits the diagnostic)
- [ ] Scope fits a 3-area review (not an org-wide redesign)
- [ ] No mismatch requiring referral (see routing rules below)
- [ ] Fit decision recorded: PROCEED / REFER / LARGER ENGAGEMENT

## Stage 3 — Booking

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Send booking link (**BOOKING LINK REQUIRED** — configure live link first)
- [ ] Send payment link for $3,500 launch price (**PAYMENT LINK REQUIRED**)
- [ ] Payment confirmed before discovery session
- [ ] Discovery session scheduled (60–90 min)
- [ ] Confirm the 3 areas to review with the buyer

## Stage 4 — Prep

**Must be reviewed before the call:**
- The intake in full, re-read
- The 3 confirmed review areas
- A working hypothesis for each area (likely strain, likely single points of failure)
- The discovery question set tailored to those areas

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Review areas confirmed and hypotheses drafted
- [ ] Discovery agenda prepared
- [ ] Note-capture template ready (see Stage 5)

## Stage 5 — Discovery call (60–90 min)

**Notes that MUST be captured during the call:**
- Who owns each reviewed workflow / decision (and who *actually* does it)
- Where work stalls, waits, or gets routed around
- Handoffs without a clear owner
- Knowledge held by single individuals
- Decision latency and approval bottlenecks
- Role overlaps / ambiguity
- Burnout/turnover signals (operational framing only — no clinical claims)
- Direct quotes and specific examples (these become the evidence base)

**Checklist:**
- [ ] All 3 areas walked through
- [ ] Single points of failure identified
- [ ] Decision bottlenecks quantified (typical latency)
- [ ] Concrete examples captured for each finding
- [ ] AI/growth intentions clarified

## Stage 6 — Diagnostic review

Synthesize notes into the People 404™ infrastructure map.

**Evidence that supports each diagnostic finding:**
- A specific example or quote from discovery
- The workflow/role/decision it maps to
- The observed severity (High / Moderate / Low)
- The downstream consequence if unaddressed

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Each finding tied to evidence (no unsupported claims)
- [ ] Severity assigned consistently
- [ ] Risk map drafted
- [ ] AI/growth readiness assessed against findings

## Stage 7 — Report build

Produce the Work Health Summary using the template in
`deliverables/people-404-work-health-summary.md`.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Executive summary + overall strain signal
- [ ] Strain signals table
- [ ] Human infrastructure risks
- [ ] Role clarity findings
- [ ] Workflow pressure points
- [ ] Knowledge transfer concerns
- [ ] Decision bottlenecks
- [ ] Burnout risk indicators (advisory framing)
- [ ] AI / growth readiness notes
- [ ] Priority recommendations
- [ ] 30-day action plan
- [ ] Suggested next step
- [ ] No overclaims: no legal/HR/clinical/compliance/ROI guarantees

## Stage 8 — Founder review

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Every finding traces to evidence
- [ ] Language matches People 404™ voice (grounded, no hype, no fearmongering)
- [ ] Disclaimer present and accurate
- [ ] 30-day plan is realistic and sequenced by leverage
- [ ] Client-specific details correct (names, areas, numbers)

## Stage 9 — Delivery

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Summary delivered (PDF or private doc — not on the public site)
- [ ] Optional follow-up call offered and scheduled if wanted
- [ ] Confirm buyer understands the plan and the first move

## Stage 10 — Follow-up

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Optional follow-up call held (pressure-test the plan, answer questions)
- [ ] Open questions resolved
- [ ] Next-step routing decided (see below)

## Stage 11 — Upsell / next-step routing

Route the outcome honestly. Only propose more work where it genuinely fits.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Outcome recorded (self-serve / larger engagement / referral / closed)
- [ ] If applicable, next engagement scoped and proposed
- [ ] Tracker updated (`trackers/people-404-tracker.md`)

---

## Routing rules

**Trigger a REFERRAL (not a Sustineri engagement) when:**
- The core need is legal advice, an HR investigation, or a compliance/regulatory audit
- The need is clinical/therapeutic mental-health support for individuals
- The problem is purely technical (software build) with no workforce-infrastructure component

**Trigger a LARGER People 404™ engagement when:**
- Findings span far more than 3 areas or the whole org
- Implementation support is wanted (not just the diagnostic + plan)
- Single points of failure require a structured knowledge-transfer program
- Role/decision redesign is needed across multiple teams

**Trigger a Sustineri OS / AI readiness offer when:**
- The buyer is actively moving to adopt/scale AI and the work system is the blocker
- Automation is planned on workflows the diagnostic flagged as "not ready as-is"
- The buyer wants an operating-system-level rebuild of how work runs, not a point fix
