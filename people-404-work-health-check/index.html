The Problem

Work breaks before people do

Most organizations can't see the strain in their own operations until it shows up as turnover, missed handoffs, or a leader who has quietly become the single point of failure. The work still gets done — because someone is absorbing the gap.

That hidden operational load doesn't appear on an org chart. It lives in the workflows people route around, the roles that were never fully defined, the knowledge that lives in one person's head, and the decisions that stall waiting for one approval.

People 404™ treats your workforce as core infrastructure — a supply chain of human work. When that infrastructure is under strain, everything built on top of it becomes fragile. The Work Health Check finds the missing pieces behind stalled work before they become failure points.

Why This Matters Now

AI readiness depends on the work system underneath it

You cannot automate, scale, or hand off a process you can't see clearly.

Before you adopt AI

AI amplifies whatever it's built on. Automating a broken workflow scales the breakage. Map the work system first.

Before you scale

Growth multiplies hidden load. Roles that were "close enough" at 12 people fracture at 40. Clarity has to come first.

Before strain compounds

Burnout, turnover, and bottlenecks are lagging signals. By the time they're visible, the infrastructure has been strained for months.

Questions

Frequently asked

How is this different from a culture survey or engagement study?

Surveys measure how people feel. The Work Health Check maps how work moves. We look at your workforce as operational infrastructure — the workflows, roles, decisions, and knowledge that carry the work — and find where that system is under strain.

Do we need to be adopting AI to benefit?

No. AI is one reason leaders come to us, but the same diagnostic applies to growth, restructuring, burnout risk, or simply wanting to see where the operational strain really is. AI readiness is a lens, not a prerequisite.

What do you need from us?

Your completed intake, access to the right people for a 60–90 minute session, and the ability to point us at up to three workflows, teams, roles, or pressure points. We do the mapping and synthesis.

Is this confidential?

Yes. Discovery notes and findings are handled privately and shared only with you. The public sample deliverable uses illustrative, anonymized content.

What happens after the Work Health Check?

You own the summary, risk map, and 30-day plan and can act on them yourself. If deeper work is warranted, we can scope a larger People 404™ engagement or a Sustineri OS / AI readiness engagement — but there's no obligation.

Why is the price $3,500?

$3,500 is the launch price for the Work Health Check (standard $5,000). It reflects a fixed-scope diagnostic — intake, a working session, the infrastructure review, and a written summary with a 30-day plan.
